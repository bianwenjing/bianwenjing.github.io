I am a DPhil student at the University of Oxford, supervised by <b>Prof. Victor Adrian Prisacariu</b> and <b>Prof. Andrea Vedaldi</b>. My research focuses on understanding the 3D world from images and videos, leveraging learning-based priors. I am particularly interested in tasks such as 3D reconstruction, 3D object detection, and visual localisation. Recently, my work has focused on exploring the application of diffusion models to 3D representations.
