I am a DPhil student at the <a href="https://www.robots.ox.ac.uk/~lav/">Active Vision Lab</a>, University of Oxford, supervised by <a href="https://www.robots.ox.ac.uk/~victor/">Prof. Victor Adrian Prisacariu</a> and <a href="https://www.robots.ox.ac.uk/~vedaldi">Prof. Andrea Vedaldi</a>. My research focuses on understanding the 3D world from images and videos, leveraging learning-based priors. I am particularly interested in tasks such as 3D reconstruction, 3D object detection, and visual localisation. Recently, my work has focused on exploring the application of diffusion models to 3D representations.

I interned at Meta Reality Labs from August 2023 to January 2024 and at Niantic Labs from July 2024 to March 2025.

Previously, I completed my MEng at Oxford (2016–2020), graduating with First-Class Honours.
